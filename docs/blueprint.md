# **App Name**: Chalke Homestay

## Core Features:

- Cinematic Atmosphere Hero: An immersive fullscreen landing experience utilizing parallax layers and slow-zoom cinematic video to establish a peaceful village atmosphere immediately upon entry.
- Eco-Luxe Room Portfolio: High-fidelity glassmorphism room cards featuring animated image carousels and smooth scroll reveal transitions for AC and Non-AC stays.
- AI Konkani Concierge Tool: A generative AI-powered tool that uses reasoning to curate personalized culinary recommendations and day-trip itineraries based on guest preferences.
- Heritage Discovery Grid: Animated travel cards with distance indicators and parallax backgrounds highlighting nearby landmarks like the Sawatsada Waterfall.
- Konkani Culinary Storytelling: A horizontal scrolling gallery dedicated to regional food culture with interactive food cards and rustic high-end visuals.
- Panoramic Terrace Visualizer: Interactive layout featuring depth-layered effects and sunset ambient lighting to preview the rooftop dining experience.
- Dynamic Reservation Interface: Floating glass booking panel with real-time field animations, floating labels, and a seamless inquiry path via WhatsApp integration.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary Evergreen (#2E6B4D) derived from the deep forest landscape to evoke serenity and growth.
- Background Alabaster Mist (#F4F9F7) providing an airy, high-contrast canvas for a premium, clean aesthetic.
- Accent Meadow Grove (#56A156) providing a natural, analogous highlight for call-to-action buttons and micro-interactions.
- Font pairing of 'Literata' (Serif) for elegant cinematic headings and 'Inter' (Sans-serif) for machine-precise body text.
- Ultra-minimal linear stroke icons featuring animated hover paths to reflect luxury homestay utility.
- Minimalist luxury layout with intentional whitespace, asymmetrical grid shifts, and layered floating cards for a curated editorial feel.
- Orchestrated motion system using Lenis smooth scroll and Framer Motion for scroll-triggered parallax and staggered content reveals.
